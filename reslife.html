Residential Life

Welcome to the Residential Life page. Here you can find information about our housing options.

Housing Options

Crampton Hall

This Hall is co-ed and is offered to junior and senior students. It is located on the upper part of campus

Ellis Hall

This Hall is co-ed and is offered to of first year and sophomore students. It is located in the center of campus.

Pixley Hall

This Hall is a womens residence hall and is offered to first year and sophomore students. It is located in the center of campus.

Turner Hall

This Hall is a mens residence hall and is offered to first year and sophomore students. It is located in the center of campus.

Gardner Hall

This Hall is a mens residence hall and is offered to first year and sophomore students. It is located in the center of campus.

Lincoln Hall

This Hall is co-ed and is offered to sophomore, junior, and senior students. It is located in the center of campus. This hall contains student academic support services in the Lincoln Hub.

Greene Hall

This Hall is co-ed suites and is offered to junior and senior students. It is located on the east side of campus.

Mundinger

This Hall is co-ed suites and is offered to junior and senior students. It is located on the east side of campus.

Fayerweather House

This campus house is co-ed and is offered to students with housing accomadations. It is located on the east side of campus.

College Ave. Apartments

The campus apartments are co-ed and are offered to junior and senior students and students with housing accomadations. They are located on the east side of campus.

Campus Houses

The campus houses are mens and womens houses and are offered to sophomore, junior, and senior students. They are located at various locations on campus.
